# Governance

Mechanical Librarian begins as a maintainer-led project. Governance should evolve as
the contributor community grows, without weakening safety or architectural
coherence.

## Decision types

- Routine implementation decisions are made through reviewed pull requests.
- Durable architecture decisions require an ADR.
- Significant product, compatibility, security, or governance changes require
  a public design discussion before implementation.
- Security-sensitive details may be discussed privately until disclosure is
  safe.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for:

- applying contribution and review rules consistently;
- protecting user data and production safety;
- keeping supported releases, migrations, and documentation accurate;
- explaining rejected proposals respectfully and concretely;
- recording durable decisions rather than relying on private context;
- avoiding vendor capture in domain contracts.

## Becoming a maintainer

Future maintainers should demonstrate sustained, constructive contributions,
sound security judgment, respect for compatibility, and willingness to do
review, documentation, and maintenance work in addition to feature work.

The maintainer nomination and removal process must be formalized before the
project has more than two maintainers.
