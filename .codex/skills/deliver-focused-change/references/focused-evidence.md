# Focused-change evidence

Keep these focused-work facts explicit in the final Markdown evidence:

- ticket and frozen update marker;
- delivery classification and why it is not a slice or defect;
- observable outcome and supported demonstration;
- every criterion with implementation and asserted-test evidence;
- changed files, documentation, migration, changelog, and compatibility impact;
- findings that clear contract, reachability, impact, and proportionality, with
  `FIX NOW`, `DEFER`, `DISPROVE`, or `OMIT` action and final status;
- each reviewer's distinct lens, attempted falsification, evidence, and residual
  uncertainty, plus the root family/remediation matrix, expansion counts, and
  miss reasons required by `.codex/finding-family-audit.md`;
- pre/post status, declared paths, explicit formatter or linter paths or `NOT
  APPLICABLE`, targeted-cohort checks, complete-gate invalidation points, and
  logical commit boundaries;
- complete local gate and demonstration results, observed remote CI, and
  unavailable #36/#37/#38-owned evidence without claiming parity;
- Project-field mutations performed, their evidence, any unresolved alignment
  conflict, and the final Project state; and
- publication challenge when required; branch, commit, pull request, closing
  reference, connector pickup/completion state, and residual risk.

End with a ticket-closure recommendation. Do not treat optional polish,
speculative hardening, or a green test name as acceptance evidence.
