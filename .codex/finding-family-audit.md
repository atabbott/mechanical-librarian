# Finding-family synthesis and remediation

The root combines independent reviewer and external evidence into bounded
remediation. Reviewers own their charter and raw evidence; they never see or
self-certify this synthesis.

## Hard review barrier

For one target/ref, wait for every applicable initial reviewer obligation to
complete under `.codex/review-method.md`. Scheduling waves may be sequential,
but no synthesis, writer packet, remediation edit, or readiness claim begins
while any applicable result is pending, unavailable, unqualified, or bound to a
different ref. Freeze the completed review set in the v3 record before editing.

## Root synthesis before editing

Build one matrix from the complete barrier:

| Family | Invariant | Sources | Manifestations and safe siblings | Contradictions | Smallest repair | Decisive checks | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stable ID | Testable sentence | Reviewer/external lineage | Affected, safe, N/A, or excluded with evidence | Direct resolution | Bounded whole-family change | Tests/probes | `FIX_NOW`, `DEFER`, `DISPROVE`, `OMIT`, `NOT_READY`, or `BLOCK` |

For every actionable row:

- trace applicable producers, consumers, callers, serializers, persistence,
  recovery, interfaces, documentation, and supported entry points;
- search by behavior as well as symbol;
- merge duplicates while preserving source lineage;
- resolve contradictions with direct contract, code, history, and executable
  evidence; and
- name uncertainty instead of inventing a sibling.

Only complete, bounded rows clearing `.codex/engineering-judgment.md` enter a
writer packet. Unbounded or contradictory families stop for reconciliation.
Do not drip-feed anchor fixes while barrier results are outstanding.

For validation and payload families, partition missing, null, wrong type,
malformed, false, unknown, duplicate, valid near-miss, ordering, and interacting
restrictions only where the frozen contract supports them. For persistence,
jobs, audit, checkpoint, migration, or recovery, inspect applicable identity,
version, fence, row cardinality, transaction scope, rollback, stale ownership,
duplicate delivery, replay, restart, and corrupt/missing evidence.

## Counters

Keep independent counters:

- `I0` is the initial pre-publication implementation review; each consolidated
  tracked correction advances `I1`, `I2`, and so on.
- `PR0` is the opening ready-PR head; each consolidated tracked PR correction
  advances `PR1`, `PR2`, and so on.
- `C1` is automatic connector review and `C2` the sole manual follow-up.

Review scheduling waves, tests, base synchronization, comments, bookkeeping,
and no-change investigations do not increment these counters.

## Mechanical family expansion

An expansion is a new, non-duplicate, material manifestation discovered after
the root recorded that family's bounded synthesis. Before any further edit,
append the finding with the same stable family ID and one miss reason:

- `CONTRACT_MISREAD`;
- `SIBLING_NOT_ENUMERATED`;
- `FIXTURE_GAP`;
- `INTERACTION_MISSED`;
- `REMEDIATION_REGRESSION`;
- `REVIEWER_CORRELATION`;
- `EXTERNAL_EVIDENCE_DISCOVERED`; or
- `OTHER`, with an explanation.

The expansion count is the chronological count of those non-duplicate
post-synthesis manifestations. Record the new count and required disposition
before opening another writer interval. Apply exactly:

1. `expansion_count = 1`: reopen and enlarge the row, record the miss, change
   investigation technique, then repair the complete expanded family.
2. `expansion_count = 2`: stop routine patching. Record root-cause and design-
   boundary analysis, choose a materially different lens, and create a required
   fresh-publication-challenge obligation before any further writer start.
3. `expansion_count >= 3`: prohibit further local patching by default. No
   writer may start until the record contains one explicit maintainer-authorized
   bounded redesign, bounded reversion, or scope decision and its constraints.

Never reset the count by renaming a family, starting another invocation,
changing `I`/`PR` stage, or marking an earlier manifestation duplicate after it
drove remediation. These thresholds do not permit an introduced defect to
remain: it is `NOT_READY` until fixed under the authorized path, reverted, or
explicitly decided.

## Remediation and same-reviewer verification

Send one writer packet containing every accepted bounded row known on the
frozen state. Require executable manifestations to fail before and pass after
repair; require decisive before/after source evidence for prose/governance.

After one coherent repair:

1. rerun the cheapest decisive checks and every evidence run invalidated by the
   edit;
2. send each affected concern to the exact original reviewer thread as a
   `TARGETED_CONTINUATION` against the actual resulting ref;
3. wait for all affected continuations or qualified unavailable-thread
   replacements before disposition or qualification;
4. have root inspect the cumulative final diff and high-risk boundaries; and
5. record new manifestations and apply the mechanical threshold before editing.

Do not reviewer-shop, ask a new reviewer to impersonate continuation, or
restart unaffected charters. If the original thread is unavailable, record the
explicit unavailable/fallback state described in `.codex/binding-preflight.md`.

## Completion

Qualification may consume current dispositions and exact-identity evidence but
reopens a family when material new evidence invalidates it. Publication requires
no unresolved `FIX_NOW`/`NOT_READY` row; a satisfied review-wave barrier;
current required private, Linux, external, local and artifact evidence; root
high-risk inspection; all required targeted continuations or qualified
unavailable-thread replacements; deterministic
preflight; and any required fresh publication challenge.
