# Code of Conduct

Mechanical Librarian should be a welcoming, technically rigorous community.

Participants must:

- treat others with respect and assume good intent while evaluating evidence;
- critique ideas and implementations rather than people;
- make space for contributors with different experience levels;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, and sharing
  another person's private information;
- respect licences, copyright, privacy, and responsible disclosure;
- keep production data and credentials out of public discussions.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or participation that
violate these expectations. Serious or repeated violations may result in a
temporary or permanent ban.

Enforcement contact information must be added before public launch.
